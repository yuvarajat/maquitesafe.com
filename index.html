About Shri Shaanthini Products

Practical hygiene solutions, supplied with care.

Shri Shaanthini Products has been wholesaling sanitary napkins since 2016, with a clear purpose: to offer practical, accessible solutions for women’s hygiene.

The Maquitesafe range includes customized hygienic napkins for varied needs. The current product literature presents the range as anti-fungal, antibacterial and chemical-free, with leak protection and eco-friendly options. Anion, bamboo and herbal choices broaden the collection, while pencil-pack “biscuit type” napkins are available for sanitary-napkin vending machines.
